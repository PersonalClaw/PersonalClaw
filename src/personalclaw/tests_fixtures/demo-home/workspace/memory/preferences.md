# User Preferences

<!-- Learned from conversations -->

#### Writing
- Summaries stay under 200 words. Anything longer gets skimmed, and a skimmed
  summary is worse than no summary.
- Every claim carries the section it came from. "Somewhere in chapter 3" is not
  a citation.
- When two sources disagree, keep both claims side by side. Do not average them
  into a sentence neither author would sign.

#### Working style
- Five items is the cap on any weekly list. A sixth means one of the five was
  not worth doing.
- Prefers being told a plan is wrong early over being handed a polished version
  of the wrong plan.
- Blocked work should say what it is waiting on, by name. "Blocked" alone is a
  dead end.

#### Infrastructure
- A backup that has never been restored counts as a hypothesis, not a backup.
- Alert thresholds that have never produced an action get raised or removed,
  not muted.
